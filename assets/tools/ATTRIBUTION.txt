Five material regions: red painted shell (3014 triangles), molded black
rubber grip (1015), brushed steel neck (2010), copper nozzle (1282), and
the front trigger surface (65). All original triangle positions are preserved
at rest. Procedural microtextures are generated locally; no external skins.
Source-to-grip coordinates (metres):
  X = (source Z - 15) / 1000
  Y = (55 - source Y) / 1000
  Z = (source X - 10) / 1000
The controller profile's neutral trigger surface and squeeze button determine
pickup translation and pitch. The trigger pivots inward by up to 6 degrees
according to analog trigger pressure (about 3.1 mm at the contact centre).
A 6 mm contact wire extends the original muzzle for seam measurement.
